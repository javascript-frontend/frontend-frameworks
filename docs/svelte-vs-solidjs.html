When Svelte wins

Pick Svelte 5 for HTML single-file components, scoped CSS, and a mature full-stack framework in SvelteKit 3.

When SolidJS wins

Pick SolidJS if your team prefers TypeScript JSX and predictable signal getter semantics without compiler magic.
