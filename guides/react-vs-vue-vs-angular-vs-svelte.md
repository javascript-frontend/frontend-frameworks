## When Each Wins

- **React 19:** Unmatched ecosystem of UI kits (Radix, shadcn/ui, TanStack), AI SDKs, and job market demand.
- **Vue 3.5:** Cleanest progressive adoption curve and unified first-party routing/state (`vue-router`, `pinia`, `nuxt`).
- **Angular 22:** Best for large enterprise teams needing opinionated structure, dependency injection, and long-term predictability.
- **Svelte 5:** Lowest boilerplate and smallest runtime footprint for fast interactive apps and SvelteKit full-stack builds.
